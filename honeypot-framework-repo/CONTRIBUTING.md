# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Honeypot Evaluation Framework.

## Weight Responses

The primary way to contribute is by sharing your professional opinion on category weights through the [Issue template](../../issues/new/choose).

**What makes a high-quality response:**
- Category weights that sum to exactly 100%
- A brief rationale explaining *why* you chose those weights
- Your professional context (role, org size, honeypot experience) — helps us segment the analysis

**What we do with your response:**
- All responses are aggregated and compared to the literature-derived base weights
- Responses may be segmented by role (e.g., "practitioners weight detection higher than academics")
- Aggregated results will be published in follow-up research
- Individual responses are public (they are GitHub Issues) but only aggregated statistics are reported in publications

## Other Contributions

- **Missing criteria?** Note them in the rationale field of your weight response, or open a separate Issue
- **Bug in the aggregation script?** Pull requests welcome
- **Framework applied to a new honeypot solution?** Open an Issue describing your evaluation — we may incorporate it as a community case study

## Code of Conduct

Be constructive and professional. Weight disagreements are expected and welcome — that is the entire point of this repository.
